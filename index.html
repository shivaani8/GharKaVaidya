🔍 Symptom guide

Search a symptom to see which nutrient shortfalls are commonly linked to it, and Indian foods that supply them.

Many symptoms have several causes. A simple blood test (CBC, ferritin, B12, vitamin D, TSH) can confirm a deficiency. Please ask a doctor before starting supplements.
⚠️ These are traditional, supportive practices — not medical advice. See a doctor for severe, sudden or lasting symptoms (e.g. severe headache with fever/vision change, heavy bleeding, blood in stool, breathlessness, joint swelling with fever). Avoid herbs and acupressure on certain points during pregnancy; check with your practitioner. Never give honey to infants under 1 year.
